=====================================
Adjusted & Flexible Work Arrangements
=====================================

*Quick Reference for Libraries & Press Staff*

Full policy: `employees.tamu.edu/employee-relations/fWA.html <https://employees.tamu.edu/employee-relations/fWA.html>`_

This is a summary of the Libraries policy and is designed to help you know
what's required. It doesn't replace the full policy; when in doubt, ask
your supervisor or the Associate University Librarian (AUL).

Key Terms
---------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Term
     - In plain terms
   * - Primary Duty Station
     - Your normal on-campus office, lab, or work site.
   * - Defined Work Schedule
     - Your agreed daily hours — usually Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm, reviewed with
       your supervisor at least twice a year.
   * - Alternate Work Location (AWL)
     - Working somewhere other than your primary duty station — either
       part-time ("partially remote") or, rarely, full-time.
   * - Fully Remote / Designated Location
     - Your job is 100% remote with no campus reporting. Rare — requires
       University Librarian & Provost approval.

Your 6 Options at a Glance
---------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 18 28 15 20 19

   * - Option
     - Use it for
     - How long
     - Who approves
     - Paperwork
   * - Adjusted Schedule
     - One-off change to your daily hours (e.g., shift 7–4 to 9–6 for the
       day)
     - Same day, within the pay period
     - Supervisor
     - Email agreement; you reply to confirm
   * - Adjusted Work Location
     - One-off remote work for a personal reason (e.g., home for a repair
       appointment)
     - A few hours up to 1 day
     - Supervisor
     - Email agreement; you reply to confirm
   * - Temporary Adjusted Work Location (TAWL)
     - Short-term remote work stint
     - More than 1 day, up to 4 weeks
     - Associate University Librarian
     - Email agreement, copy to Director
   * - Temporary Remote Work
     - Working at another TAMU library/office for a special project
     - As directed by your administrator
     - Work administrator
     - Calendar entry: "Working Elsewhere"
   * - Flexible Work Schedule
     - An ongoing, different daily/weekly schedule than the standard one
     - Ongoing; reviewed at least yearly
     - Manager (formal memo)
     - Signed memo + entered in Workday
   * - Alternate Work Location (ongoing)
     - Regularly working remote up to 2 days/week
     - Ongoing; reviewed at least yearly
     - Manager (formal memo)
     - Signed memo + entered in Workday

Ground Rules for All Options
-----------------------------

- Must fit your job duties — some roles simply can't flex, and that's OK.
- You must be in good standing (not under discipline, and your last review
  was at least "Meets Expectations"). Rare exceptions for the two one-off
  options require AUL + University Librarian approval.
- None of these replace using Leave — they're for short, specific needs,
  not ongoing absences.
- Your supervisor can pause or end an arrangement anytime business needs
  require it.

While Working Flex or Remote, You're Expected To…
----------------------------------------------------

- Reply within ~20 minutes to a direct message if you should be at your
  workstation (calendar open, no meeting, not at lunch).
- Start on an assigned task within ~45 minutes of being asked.
- Keep your calendar current and shared — block time and note why if
  you're unreachable during your schedule.
- Be as available as if you were in the office, and come in when a
  business need requires it.
