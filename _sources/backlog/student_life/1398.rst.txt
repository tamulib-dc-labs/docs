Scope and Content
-----------------

This scrapbook contains memorabilia, photographs, and biographical information from when Edgar Petty Jennings was at A&M College from 1912 to 1917.

Materials inside include:

THE BATTALION

* Special Edition Company "A"

AUTOGRAPHS

* Date, Name, Home, Birthday, A Word or 2
* Inter-Collegiate Record Award
* Football (1914-1915)

RIBBON

* 2nd Southwest Intercollegiate Conference, Track and Field Meet (May 13th, 1916)

NEWSPAPER CLIPPINGS

* Ceremonies in Honor of American
* Sailor and Marines Killed at Veracruz
* Photographs: Classmates, Burning of Old Main in 1912, etc.

FRAT CHAT

FORMS

* Record of a Year's Business on a farm

PROGRAM

* Commencement Exercises (1914)
