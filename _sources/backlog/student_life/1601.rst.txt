Scope and Content
-----------------

This scrapbook contains photographs, programs and publications collected by Louis A. Hartung, Class of 1929 during his time at Texas A&M from 1925 to 1929.

Materials inside include:

AUTOGRAPHS

SPORT

* Intramural athletic association (constitution and bylaws)
* Football tickets (1925)
* Football programs (1925)
* Football photographs
* Basketball, track, baseball

PHOROGRAPHS

* Football
* Company B
* Aerial campus view
* Austin Corps
* Academic Building

PUBLICATIONS

* "An Open Letter to College Freshman"
* "Fish Don'ts"
* "Father's Idea of How his Son Spends the Day at A&M"
* "There Goes the Grange"
* "Westinghouse Light and Power Plants"
* "Fairbanks - Morse"

PROGRAMS

* Review of Cadet Corps Honoring Visiting Railroad Officials
* 50th Annual Commencement Exercises
* Presentation of Commissions
* 8th Annual Rodeo and Pageant
* Choral Club of Baylor College
* Patriotic Program and Banquet
