=====================================
Registering ISSNs for Hosted Journals
=====================================

This page describes how to request an International Standard Serial Number
(ISSN) from the U.S. ISSN Center at the Library of Congress for journals hosted
on the Libraries' OJS service. It covers pre-publication (provisional) requests,
the ISSN Uplink application, and the follow-up needed to confirm a provisional
ISSN after launch.


Overview
========

* ISSNs for serials published in the United States are assigned by the
  `U.S. ISSN Center <https://www.loc.gov/issn/>`_ at the Library of Congress.
* Assignment is free.
* All applications are submitted through
  `ISSN Uplink <https://www.loc.gov/issn/>`_. Uplink replaced all earlier
  application methods in November 2020.
* Each format needs its own ISSN. A journal published both online and in
  print needs two ISSNs, one for each format. Both formats are requested on a
  single application.
* Processing can take **up to six weeks**. Build this into the launch
  timeline, especially when the ISSN is needed before DOI registration with
  Crossref.


Pre-publication (provisional) ISSNs
===================================

A pre-publication ISSN is assigned before the first issue appears. It
remains *provisional* in the `ISSN Portal <https://portal.issn.org/>`_
until the U.S. ISSN Center reviews a published issue.

Library of Congress conditions
------------------------------

The U.S. ISSN Center assigns pre-publication ISSNs only when:

* No issues or content have been published under the title.
* The first issue is no more than **six months** away. The application form
  will not offer dates further out.
* A mock-up of the publication is provided with the application.
* The applicant accepts that the request may be deferred until the first
  issue is published.

.. note::

   ISSN International Centre policy generally does not assign pre-publication
   ISSNs to online-only resources. The U.S. center makes an exception for
   historically well-established publishing organizations. Requests from the
   Libraries for OJS-hosted journals have been granted provisional ISSNs on
   this basis. State clearly in the application that the journal is published
   and hosted by Texas A&M University Libraries.

.. important::

   Open access journals must publish an issue with **at least five articles**
   before an ISSN can be confirmed. Plan the first issue accordingly.

If a journal does not qualify for a pre-publication ISSN, print
"ISSN pending" on the publication and apply after the first issue is
published.


Before you start
================

Gather the following before opening the application. **Attachments added to a
saved draft are not retained**, so add them only when ready to submit.

* Final journal title, plus any acronym or short form.
* Expected date and numbering of the first issue, e.g.
  ``Vol. 1, no. 1 (Spring 2027)``.
* Publisher name. See :ref:`issn-publisher-name`.
* The journal's OJS landing page URL. Make it live, even as a placeholder,
  so the URL is stable.
* Mock-up files (PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX, or RTF; 1 GB maximum) showing:

  * the journal homepage with the title displayed
  * the About or masthead page with publisher, editorial board, frequency,
    and the open access and licensing statement
  * a mock table of contents for the first issue
  * a sample article first page or galley header showing where the title
    and ISSN will appear


Completing the Uplink application
=================================

Fields marked with an asterisk are required. Leave optional fields blank
rather than entering "n/a" or "unknown".

Title
-----

Title of Publication
   The exact title as it will appear on the OJS masthead.

Variation of the title
   Any acronym or alternate form the journal will be known by.

Previous/former title
   Leave blank for a new journal.

Formats
-------

Select **Online** for OJS journals. Also select **Print** if readers can obtain
print copies in any way, including print-on-demand. Every format selected
needs its own sample attachments.

Publisher
---------

Type of publication
   Journal.

Date and number of first issuance of this title
   For example, ``Vol. 1, no. 1 (Spring 2027)``.

Publisher Name
   See :ref:`issn-publisher-name`.

Country, State, City of Publication
   United States / Texas / College Station.

Is there a previous publisher?
   No, for a new journal.

.. _issn-publisher-name:

Choosing the publisher name
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Library of Congress defines the publisher as the organization responsible
for the *content*, not the printer, distributor, or hosting platform.

* If the Libraries publishes the journal, enter **Texas A&M University
  Libraries**.
* If a department, society, or editorial board is responsible for the content
  and the Libraries only hosts it, enter that body as publisher. Note the
  Libraries' hosting role in *Additional notes*.

Contact
-------

Enter the contact name, email, phone, and business address. Add teammates to
the publishing team only if they need notifications for this title. Every team
member receives every message about the application.

Documentation
-------------

Direct URL for this title
   The journal's OJS landing page.

Additional notes
   Include:

   * that this is a pre-publication request, with the expected launch date
     and publication frequency
   * that the journal is published and/or hosted by Texas A&M University
     Libraries
   * for open access journals, that the first issue will contain at least
     five articles

Attachments
   Upload the mock-up files listed in `Before you start`_.


After the ISSN is assigned
==========================

#. Add the ISSN to the journal settings in OJS so it appears on the site
   and in exported metadata. Label each ISSN with its format if there is more
   than one, e.g. ``ISSN 1234-567X (Online)``.
#. Share the ISSN with the editors for use on article galleys and
   promotional material.
#. Record the ISSN with the journal's other identifiers, such as its Crossref
   DOI prefix and title record.


Confirming a provisional ISSN
=============================

After the first issue is published:

#. Log in to ISSN Uplink.
#. Complete the **Update publication status** form for the title and give the
   URL of the published issue as proof of publication.
#. Wait for the ISSN Center to review the issue. Uplink sends a reminder if the
   expected publication date passes without an update.
#. The application status changes to *ISSN record updated*. The ISSN Portal
   shows the ISSN as *confirmed* about 7–10 business days later.

Changes before launch
---------------------

Use the **Update publication status** form in Uplink for any of these:

Publication delayed
   The provisional ISSN can be kept. Enter the new expected date.

Title changed before the first issue
   The same ISSN can still be used. Enter the new title.

Journal will not launch
   Report the cancellation, and the ISSN Center will cancel the ISSN.

.. warning::

   A title change *after* publication requires a new ISSN. Apply at least a
   month before the change takes effect.
