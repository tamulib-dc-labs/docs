==================
Mission and Vision
==================

-------
Mission
-------

Digital Collections Services preserves, manages, and provides open access to the scholarly, creative,
and cultural heritage record of Texas A&M University, building repository infrastructure and applying
emerging technologies so that this record remains discoverable, trustworthy, and usable for generations
to come.

------
Vision
------

We envision a Texas A&M University where every scholar, researcher, and community member can find,
access, and build upon the University's digital scholarship and cultural heritage, supported by
repository systems that are resilient, interoperable, and enriched by responsible innovation in
artificial intelligence and machine learning.

--------------
What Guides Us
--------------

* **Access** — We remove barriers to discovery and use, from open access publishing to accessible,
  standards-compliant digital collections and exhibits.
* **Stewardship** — We are long-term stewards of the University's scholarly output and cultural
  heritage materials, committed to their preservation and integrity.
* **Innovation** — We responsibly evaluate and adopt new technologies, including AI and machine
  learning, to increase the scale, accuracy, and impact of our work.
* **Collaboration** — We partner across the Libraries and Special Collections, campus units such as HPRC and technology services,
  and external organizations such as the Texas Digital Library to deliver services no
  single team could sustain alone.
* **Openness** — We favor open access, open scholarship, open source, open standards, and transparent practices that expand who can
  participate in and benefit from Texas A&M's research and cultural record.
