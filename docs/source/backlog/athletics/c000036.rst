Scope and Content
-----------------

This collection contains gameday programs for the Texas A&M Football team.

System of Arrangement
---------------------

The collection is arranged chronologically by season, and then chronologically within the season.

Notes
-----

Missing and Needed

* Texas A&M vs. Manhattan College, December 1936, reported missing in summer 1994.

* Texas A&M vs. Manhattan College, October 2, 1937, reported missing in summer 1994.

* Texas A&M vs. Miami University, 1944-12-08, reported missing March 5, 2015.

* Texas A&M vs Rice, November 14, 1953, reported missing March 5, 2015.

* Texas A&M vs Houston, October 9, 1954, reported missing March 5, 2015.

* Texas A&M vs Texas, December 1, 1990, reported missing April 2, 2015

* 2004 Season - box 38 is a placeholder for the 2004 season we currently do not have programs from.

* 2015-2016 Season (partial) - box 48 is a placeholder for the parts of the 2015 and 2016 season that we currently do not have programs from.

* Other seasons we currently do not have:

* 1907-1915

* 1918

* 1920

* 1928

* 1930-1932

* 1934
