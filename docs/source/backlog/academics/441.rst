Scope and Content
-----------------

This collection includes the following publication titles:
* *Focal Point* (1960s)
* *ARCC Newsletter* Architectural Research Center Consortium (1987-1989)
* *Right Angle* (1990-1996)
* *Communique* (1983-1985)
* *Synthesis: A Department Newsletter* (1982-1983, 1985, 1987-1988)
* Landscape Architecture Department Newsletter (1982-1987)
* TAMU Student Chapter ASLA *Workshop '84*
* *Research Notes* (1986-1987)
* *CARC News*; The Newsletter of Texas A&M University's College of Architecture (Winter 1999)
