Scope and Content
-----------------

This scrapbook contains autographs, memorabilia, and photographs of A&M College buildings during the span of 1917 to 1921 when Finn was a student at the College.

Materials inside include:

* Autographs
* Old A-2, photographs of individuals
* Football photographs - 1917
* Building photographs - E.E. Building, Extension Building, Main Building, Guion Hall, Leggett Hall, Vet Building, Stock Judging Barn, Foster Hall, Chemistry Building, Goodwin Hall, Textile Engineering Building, YMCA, CE Building, Print Shop, Dorms, Campus Views.
* Women, photographs
* Athletics - football, baseball, track, tennis, basketball
